# Credentials and challenges directory (plan, not built)

Status: outline to revisit with the owner (2026-11-06). Nothing here is implemented yet. Names below are from general
knowledge and must be checked against each organization's current site before anything ships (programs rename, retire and
change requirements often).

## What it is

A browsable directory inside Dragonfly Coach of things people in fitness can **earn**, with what each takes and how to work
toward it. Two audiences:

1. **Trainers and instructors**: professional certifications and continuing-education credentials.
2. **Clients**: public standards and challenges they can train for (and their trainer can program toward).

Each entry links out to the official source; Dragonfly does not sell or award these and makes no affiliation claims.

## Entry shape (draft)

- Name, issuing organization, official link
- Audience (trainer / client / both) and discipline (strength, kettlebell, Pilates, yoga, aquatics, group fitness, nutrition,
  corrective, coaching, safety)
- What it proves, prerequisites, format (online exam, in-person workshop, practical test), renewal / CEUs
- The standard itself when public (for example a timed kettlebell test), with related library exercises
- "Working toward it" tools: a template program, the related exercises, a progress tracker
- Last verified date (directory items go stale; show it)

## Starter list to verify (examples, not final)

**Personal training (accredited CPTs)**: NASM, ACE, ACSM, NSCA (CPT, CSCS), ISSA, NCCPT, NFPT.
**Specializations**: NASM CES / PES; Precision Nutrition Level 1/2; FMS / SFMA; TRX courses; Animal Flow; DVRT.
**Kettlebell and strength**: StrongFirst (SFG I/II, SFL, SFB), RKC, USA Weightlifting coaching levels, powerlifting coaching
courses, CrossFit Level 1-4.
**Pilates**: comprehensive and mat programs (for example Balanced Body, STOTT/Merrithew, BASI), PMA certification exam.
**Yoga**: Yoga Alliance registered levels (RYT 200 / 500) and specialty trainings.
**Group fitness**: AFAA / ACE group fitness, Les Mills program certifications (BODYPUMP and others are Les Mills
trademarks; link, do not copy choreography), indoor cycling (for example Spinning, Schwinn), Zumba.
**Aquatics**: aquatic fitness instructor certifications (for example AEA), lifeguarding.
**Safety (often required)**: CPR / AED / First Aid (American Red Cross, American Heart Association).

**Client standards and challenges** (public tests people train for): StrongFirst Simple & Sinister timed standard,
kettlebell snatch tests used in certifications, bodyweight milestones (first pull-up, strict muscle-up, pistol squat),
strength milestones (bodyweight bench, 2x bodyweight deadlift), running distances (5K to marathon), military / first
responder fitness tests, sanctioned powerlifting and weightlifting meets.

## How it could fit the app

- New "Grow" or "Credentials" area beside the exercise library; searchable and filterable like the library.
- Trainers mark what they hold (shown on their profile, optional) and what they're working toward.
- Clients pick a challenge; the trainer can attach a template program and track progress toward the standard.
- Data lives online (like library additions) so it can be updated without an app release; same report button for
  outdated entries.
